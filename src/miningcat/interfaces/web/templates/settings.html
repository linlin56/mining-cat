Import a {{ study.name }} dictionary

MiningCat reads dictionaries in Yomitan's format (.zip). For example CC-CEDICT (Mandarin), words.hk (Cantonese), JMdict (Japanese), and frequency lists. They're stored in the project's library/ folder.

{{ study.name }} frequency list

Recommended sentences (i+1) only teach frequent words: their new word must be high enough in your frequency list. The more of its words you know, the further down the list they go. The list's rank is also shown in the dictionary popup and the card creator.
Import a Yomitan frequency dictionary (.zip), or a JSON frequency list (.json, the words from the most frequent; a text file with a word per line works too).

Your {{ study.name }} words

Connection

MiningCat talks to Anki through the AnkiConnect add-on: in Anki, open Tools › Add-ons › Get Add-ons… and enter the code 2055492159, then restart Anki. When Anki is closed, your cards wait here and are sent at the next sync.

New cards

Choose where your {{ study.name }} cards go and what each field of your note type receives.

Sentence translation

New cards get a translation of their sentence, made offline with Argos Translate. The first time a language is translated, its model (about 100 MB) is downloaded.

Models

Download the {{ study.name }} models ahead of time, so that the first card doesn't wait for them. {% if study.id == "zh" %}The models of the characters you learn (traditional, simplified or both) are downloaded.{% endif %}

Word statuses from Anki

At each sync, MiningCat reads the cards of these {{ study.name }} decks: a word is learning while its card is young, and known once its interval reaches the number of days below. Words you marked known or ignored yourself are never changed. Cards made in MiningCat are always followed.

{{ study.name }} cards

Cards waiting for Anki are sent at the next sync. If you can't use AnkiConnect (on another computer, or with AnkiDroid / AnkiMobile), export them to an .apkg file and open it with Anki: they use a “MiningCat” note type.

Theme

The colours of every page, kept in user/user-config.json. The reader's own theme (sepia…) is set in its settings, while a book is read.

Highlight colors

The colours of the words by status (new, learning), of the recommended sentences (i+1) and of the word looked up, in the reader, the player, the comics, the clipboard and the video game captures. Kept in user/user-config.json.
