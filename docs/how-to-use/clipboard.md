# Clipboard

The **Clipboard** reads any text without making a file of it: an article, a chat message, song lyrics...

1. Open **Clipboard** from the home page (or go to `/clipboard/`).
2. Type or paste your text in the page.
3. Click **Read** (or press `Ctrl` / `⌘` + `Enter`).

The text then works like a book of the [Reader](reader.md): click a word to look it up in your [dictionaries](mining.md), change its status, and make an Anki card with its sentence. Words are coloured by status as you scroll, so long texts open right away. Click **Edit** to change the text.

The text is kept in your browser: it's still there the next time you open the page. **Clear** empties it. For Chinese, the menu in the header tells whether the text is in traditional or simplified characters.
