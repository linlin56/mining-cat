Import a dictionary

MiningCat reads dictionaries in Yomitan's format (.zip). For example CC-CEDICT (Mandarin), words.hk (Cantonese), JMdict (Japanese), and frequency lists. They're stored in the project's library/ folder.

Your dictionaries

Results are shown in this order. Disable a dictionary to hide it without deleting it.

Chinese characters

Choose the characters you learn. Words are saved in that script; a word whose characters are the same in both scripts counts for both. When you know a word in the other script, the dictionary tells you.

Your words

Each language has its own words: knowing 說 in Mandarin doesn't mean you know it in Cantonese.

Connection

MiningCat talks to Anki through the AnkiConnect add-on: in Anki, open Tools › Add-ons › Get Add-ons… and enter the code 2055492159, then restart Anki. When Anki is closed, your cards wait here and are sent at the next sync.

New cards

For each language, choose where cards go and what each field of your note type receives.

Sentence translation

New cards get a translation of their sentence, made offline with Argos Translate. The first time a language is translated, its model (about 100 MB) is downloaded.

Word statuses from Anki

At each sync, MiningCat reads the cards of these decks: a word is learning while its card is young, and known once its interval reaches the number of days below. Words you marked known or ignored yourself are never changed. Cards made in MiningCat are always followed.

Cards

Cards waiting for Anki are sent at the next sync. If you can't use AnkiConnect (on another computer, or with AnkiDroid / AnkiMobile), export them to an .apkg file and open it with Anki: they use a “MiningCat” note type.
